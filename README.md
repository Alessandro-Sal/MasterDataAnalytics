# EPICODE - Master in Data Analytics & AI

Welcome to my repository for the **Master in Data Analytics and Artificial Intelligence** at **EPICODE School of Technology**. 

## 📖 About this Repository

This workspace serves as an archive of my academic journey and a portfolio of my practical skills. Inside, you will find all the coursework completed during the master's program, which is divided into two main areas:

*   **Class Exercises:** Daily and weekly coding assignments and tasks requested by the professors to practice and consolidate new theoretical concepts.
*   **End-of-Module Projects:** Comprehensive capstone projects developed at the end of each module. These projects simulate real-world scenarios and integrate all the analytical and AI skills acquired during the module.
